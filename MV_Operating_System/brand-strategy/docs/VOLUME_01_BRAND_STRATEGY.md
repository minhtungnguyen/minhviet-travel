# FOREWORD

Minh Việt is not merely building a travel company. Minh Việt is building an organization capable of designing, operating and continuously improving meaningful journeys through professional service, technology, data and artificial intelligence.

Travel is the starting point, not the final boundary. The same operating philosophy can support tourism, MICE, booking, destination platforms, customer experience systems and AI-enabled business services.

This volume establishes the strategic constitution of the brand. It is intended to guide people, products, systems and AI Agents over the long term.

# CHAPTER 1 - WHY WE EXIST

## Objective

Define the fundamental reason Minh Việt exists beyond selling products or generating revenue.

Customers do not merely buy tours, tickets, hotels or events. They buy confidence, reduced uncertainty, professional coordination, meaningful memories and a dependable partner.

Every product, campaign, process and system must answer:

- A corporate annual trip
- A MICE or gala program

- Design the complete experience, not only the transaction.
- Reduce uncertainty before, during and after service delivery.
- Assign clear responsibility.

## Don't

- Treat customers as booking numbers.
- Optimize only for immediate conversion.
- End the relationship after payment.
- add technology without clear customer value.

# PART IV - BRAND GOVERNANCE

# CHAPTER 13 - BRAND NON-NEGOTIABLES

## Purpose

Protect the brand from decisions that may benefit a department or short-term target while damaging long-term trust and coherence.

## Twelve non-negotiables

### 1. Trust Before Revenue

Do not hide mandatory costs, use misleading visuals, exaggerate quality, create false scarcity or commit before verification.

### 2. Customer Fit Before Product Push

Understand purpose, constraints, concerns and non-negotiable criteria before recommending.

### 3. Experience Before Technology

Technology must improve understanding, decisions, convenience, safety or support.

### 4. Human Judgment Where It Matters

Humans retain authority for final booking, price exceptions, refunds, complaints, legal matters, personal data, high-risk cases and important commitments.

### 5. Accuracy Before Speed

When data is incomplete, state that verification is required.

### 6. Clear Ownership

Every lead, booking, contract, incident and complaint must have one accountable owner.

### 7. One Source of Truth

Use canonical systems for products, prices, CRM, documents, brand knowledge, SOPs, feedback and trust assets.

### 8. Privacy by Design

Collect only necessary data and control access, retention and use.

### 9. Consistency Across Every Touchpoint

Website, advertising, Sales, Operations, AI, vouchers and contracts must align.

### 10. Quality Must Be Operational

Quality must be converted into checklists, standards, ownership, control points and learning loops.

### 11. Long-Term Value Before Short-Term Attention

Do not trade trust for traffic, exaggeration or low-quality leads.

### 12. Learn from Every Journey

Feed lessons into CRM, Knowledge Base, SOPs, product design, training and AI.

## Priority order for AI and operational decisions

  ↓

Customer interest

# CHAPTER 14 - BRAND DECISION FRAMEWORK

## The MV Seven-Gate Decision Model

Every strategic initiative passes seven gates.

### Gate 1 - Strategic Alignment

Does it support Vision 2035, core capabilities and current priorities without unacceptable distraction?

### Gate 2 - Customer Value

Which persona has which problem, and what measurable value is created?

Customer Problem Statement template:

> Nhóm khách hàng [Persona] đang gặp [vấn đề] trong [bối cảnh], khiến họ [hậu quả]. Giải pháp này giúp họ [giá trị cụ thể].

### Gate 3 - Brand Fit

Does it reinforce Brand Promise, The Guide + The Sage, professionalism, trust and companionship?

### Gate 4 - Operational Feasibility

Are Owner, process, resources, quality standards, exception handling and stop criteria clear?

### Gate 5 - Data & Technology Readiness

Are source data, integration, access control, audit log, backup, recovery, data ownership and scale addressed?

Rules:

- Do not build an app before understanding data.
- Do not deploy AI without a reliable Knowledge Base.
- Do not automate a chaotic process.

### Gate 6 - Economics

Does it create revenue, reduce cost or create a monetizable strategic asset? Evaluate margins, CAC, CLV, payback, infrastructure and support costs.

### Gate 7 - Risk, Ethics & Reversibility

Assess worst-case impact, legal/privacy risk, reputational exposure, reversibility and exit cost.

## Decision types

- Type 1: hard to reverse - slow, deeply verified, executive approval.
- Type 2: easy to reverse - test quickly, measure and learn.

## Scorecard

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Strategic Alignment | 15 |
| Customer Value | 20 |
| Brand Fit | 15 |
| Operational Feasibility | 15 |
| Data & Technology Readiness | 10 |
| Economics | 15 |
| Risk & Reversibility | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

Decision thresholds:

- 80-100: Approve
- 65-79: Pilot
- 50-64: Revise
- Below 50: Reject or Park

## Hard stops

Stop regardless of score if there is serious customer deception, missing ownership, unlawful data use, major privacy risk, uncontrolled AI authority, no recovery plan, unacceptable supplier quality, unclear economic value or damage to the core brand.

## Current-stage priority order

1. Revenue-generating capability
2. Data import and management
3. Lead capture and CRM
4. Sales and nurturing
5. Operations
6. Measurement
7. Advanced AI
8. Impressive features without direct value

## Required decision output

- Approve, Pilot, Revise or Reject
- Rationale
- KPI
- Owner
- Risks
- Stop criteria
- Review date

# CHAPTER 15 - AI BRAND CONSTITUTION

## Official role of AI

> A system that helps people understand customers, manage knowledge, operate efficiently and make better decisions.

AI is not the final legal, financial, booking or crisis authority.

## Permitted roles

1. Advisor
2. Assistant
3. Analyst
4. Coordinator
5. Knowledge Interface

## Sixteen constitutional articles

1. **Truthfulness** - never fabricate facts or actions.
2. **Source Grounding** - important answers must use approved sources.
3. **Confidence Disclosure** - classify Confirmed, Likely, Unknown or Requires Human Verification.
4. **No Autonomous Commitment** - no autonomous booking, ticketing, refunds, legal or commercial commitments.
5. **Human Escalation** - transfer complaints, safety, legal, high-value, ambiguous and out-of-policy cases.
6. **Customer Interest** - recommend by suitability, not merely margin.
7. **Data Minimization** - request only necessary data at the appropriate workflow stage.
8. **Privacy & Confidentiality** - never leak personal, supplier-cost or internal data.
9. **Role-Based Access** - least-privilege permissions.
10. **Brand Voice Compliance** - professional, calm, clear and non-manipulative.
11. **No Manipulation** - no fake scarcity, fear, dark patterns or fabricated social proof.
12. **Explainability** - explain criteria, data, limitations and assumptions.
13. **Fairness** - use service-relevant factors, not inappropriate sensitive inference.
14. **Auditability** - log important inputs, sources, outputs, actions, approvals and versions.
15. **Continuous Evaluation** - test accuracy, hallucination, escalation, completion, satisfaction, policy compliance and cost.
16. **Human Accountability** - every Agent has business, technical and data ownership.

## AI risk levels

- Level 1 - Low: drafts, summaries, classification, hashtags, knowledge search.
- Level 2 - Moderate: recommendations, lead scoring, itinerary suggestions, comparison.
- Level 3 - High: pricing, refund policy, visa conditions, complaints, personal data, booking changes - mandatory human-in-the-loop.
- Level 4 - Prohibited autonomous use: contracts, transfers, legal commitments, critical deletion, admin-right changes, crisis announcements, sensitive-data disclosure, safety/medical decisions or impersonation.

## Escalation protocol

```text
Detect risk
  ↓
Stop autonomous action
  ↓
Explain limitation
  ↓
Capture context
  ↓
Route to correct owner
  ↓
Track status
  ↓
Confirm receipt
```

## AI memory rule

Long-term memory may include useful, non-sensitive preferences with a clear purpose. Do not retain passwords, card details, full identity documents or unnecessary sensitive records.

## AI Agent Identity Card

Every Agent must declare purpose, audience, owners, sources, permissions, prohibited actions, risk level, escalation points, KPI, tests and version.
