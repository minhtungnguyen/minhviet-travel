# CLAUDE.md — Volume 02 Addendum

When working on UI, UX, frontend, dashboards, CMS, CRM, public websites, mobile interfaces or AI interfaces:

1. Read Volume 01 Brand Strategy first.
2. Read `docs/VOLUME_02_DESIGN_OPERATING_SYSTEM.md`.
3. Use semantic design tokens from `tokens/design-tokens.json`.
4. Follow `standards/COMPONENT_RULES.md`.
5. Do not code from screenshots alone.
6. Define loading, empty, error, success and permission states.
7. Define mobile behavior.
8. Preserve accessibility and keyboard behavior.
9. Do not create fake scarcity, testimonials, ratings or precision.
10. Do not hard-code business content that belongs in CMS or product data.
11. Reuse components before creating new ones.
12. For AI interfaces, apply Volume 01 AI Brand Constitution and Chapter 17 of Volume 02.
13. Before modifying files, summarize:
   - user job;
   - primary CTA;
   - applicable brand rules;
   - component reuse plan;
   - data requirements;
   - risk and accessibility considerations.
14. After implementation, run the Design QA checklist.
